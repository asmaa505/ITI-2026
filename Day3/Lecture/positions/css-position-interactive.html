Quick reference

    

      
static

      
Default flow. Offsets and z-index are ignored entirely.

    

    

      
relative

      
Offset from its own normal slot; the original space stays reserved.

    

    

      
absolute

      
Out of flow; placed against the nearest positioned ancestor.

    

    

      
fixed

      
Out of flow; placed against the viewport, ignoring scroll.

    

    

      
sticky

      
Relative, then locks like fixed once a scroll threshold is passed.
